Work with me

I'm an independent field researcher based in Taipei. I design and run qualitative interview studies, and I'm currently open to contract or part-time projects.

What I do

  • Street-intercept and face-to-face interview fieldwork, in Chinese and English
  • Interview guide design and iteration in the field
  • Coding and synthesis of open-ended responses
  • Research record-keeping and data-minimization practices, including access tiers and location-data handling

What I'm looking for

  • Contract or part-time qualitative research, especially interview fieldwork in Taiwan and in Chinese–English bilingual settings
  • Analysis and synthesis support for interview data
  • Research operations and documentation

Evidence

  • 328 valid interviews over 114 field days, with participants from 33 nationalities, Taiwan included (full study · working paper)
  • Field and technical notes are public (notes collection)
  • Limitations are stated openly in the paper (Section 3.8)

How I work

  • Written-first and asynchronous; based in UTC+8 and happy to schedule calls across APAC and Europe
  • I use AI tools for drafting and analysis, and I review every output myself
  • Independent and self-funded, with no institutional affiliation
