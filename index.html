My Core Identities

  • AI Collaboration Explorer: Exploring the possibilities and future development of human-machine collaboration.
  • Street Observer: I enjoy observing people's interactions with technology in open spaces — MRT stations, department stores, parks.
  • Photography Enthusiast: Through the lens I pursue compositional extremes, which resonates with my drive for logical coherence.
  • Independent Researcher: Self-funded, unaffiliated. I believe research conducted under this level of resource constraint offers a kind of evidentiary value that institutional research — designed around adequate funding and institutional support — has little reason to replicate.

My thinking pattern is highly distinctive — I tend toward de-gendered and de-aged rational analysis. I have walked this path of collaboration largely alone, hoping to build an "AI ethics safety mechanism" capable of protecting the evolution of human civilization.

I am not an engineer, and not a teacher. I am simply someone who wants to know where the limits are.
